# Introduction to Ethics

A 7-lesson introductory college philosophy course with weekly case
discussions and short argument-reconstruction exercises.

## Weekly topics
1. What is ethics? Moral claims versus preferences and law.
2. Relativism and objectivity: can moral claims be true?
3. Utilitarianism: consequences, welfare, and the trolley problem.
4. Deontology: duties, rights, and Kant's categorical imperative.
5. Virtue ethics: character, flourishing, and the golden mean.
6. Applied ethics I: lying, promises, and everyday dilemmas.
7. Applied ethics II: a capstone case analysis using all three frameworks.

## Outcomes
Students can reconstruct a moral argument, apply utilitarian, deontological,
and virtue-ethical reasoning to a novel case, and defend a position in writing
while fairly stating the strongest objection.
