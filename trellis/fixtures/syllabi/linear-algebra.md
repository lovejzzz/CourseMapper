# Linear Algebra

Linear Algebra, a 14-lesson undergraduate course with weekly problem sets and
two exams. Lessons cover: systems of linear equations and row reduction;
vectors and linear combinations; matrix algebra and matrix multiplication;
the inverse of a matrix and invertibility; determinants and their properties;
vector spaces and subspaces; linear independence, basis, and dimension; a
midterm exam covering systems through subspaces; column space, null space,
and rank; linear transformations and their matrices; eigenvalues and
eigenvectors; diagonalization; orthogonality, projections, and least squares;
and a comprehensive review with a final exam.

Grading: weekly problem sets 30%, midterm exam 30%, final exam 40%.
