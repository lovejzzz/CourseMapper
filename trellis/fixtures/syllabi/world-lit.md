# World Literature

World Literature, a 14-lesson undergraduate seminar with weekly reading
responses and close-reading checks; named primary texts are expected
throughout. Lessons cover: what counts as world literature; the oral epic
tradition with Gilgamesh and Homer; classical drama with Sophocles; Tang
poetry with Li Bai and Du Fu; the Thousand and One Nights and frame
narratives; Dante; comparative reading methods culminating in a comparative
essay proposal; translation and cultural mediation; postcolonial literature
with Achebe; magical realism with García Márquez; modernist poetry; the
fantastic with Borges; contemporary global fiction; and a final paper with
course synthesis.

Grading: weekly reading responses 30%, comparative essay proposal 10%,
midterm close-reading exam 25%, final paper 35%.
