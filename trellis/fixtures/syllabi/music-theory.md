# Music Theory Fundamentals

A 7-lesson introductory college course with weekly listening and notation
exercises.

## Weekly topics

1. The staff, clefs, and pitch notation.
2. Intervals and how to hear them.
3. Major and minor scales and key signatures.
4. Triads and seventh chords.
5. Rhythm, meter, and time signatures.
6. Harmony and basic chord progressions.
7. Musical form, with a final analysis project.

## Outcomes

Students can read pitches in treble and bass clef, identify intervals and
triads by ear and on paper, spell any major or minor scale, and analyze the
form and harmony of a short piece.
