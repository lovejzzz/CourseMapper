## Lane 2 — CALM: one count, a two-number seal, a quieter crown

### B1. ONE review count (the screenshot inconsistency)

The v0.14.8 screenshot shows the header saying **"Review 3"** while the panel chips counted **26** (1 observation + 23 spot-checks + 2 structural). Root cause: AppFlow's header queue omits `reviewItems` (the spot-check checklist) that the panel includes — two builders, two truths. The fix is definitional, not just plumbing:

- **The headline count = items needing JUDGMENT** (observations + structural + pending sync). Spot-checks are routine confirmations — they appear in the drawer under their own class with a "confirm all" affordance, but they don't inflate the headline number an instructor sees.
- One selector builds both surfaces (AppFlow passes its queue down; the panel stops building its own), so the number CANNOT diverge again — one fact, one place, machine-checked by a test that renders both from the same fixture.
- **Bar:** header CTA, drawer, and agent panel all derive from one queue object; a matrix test pins the headline-vs-drawer count semantics.

### B2. The two-number Seal (texture comes out of hiding)

The grader has carried a calibrated texture dimension since v0.14.7 — computed on every package, shown nowhere. The founding doc's Seal is two numbers because one perfect number hides a 5/10. Surface it:

- The header chip becomes "Quality 100 · Texture 74" (texture clearly labeled advisory in the tooltip + report; slate not emerald — it's a meter, not a grade).
- QUALITY_REPORT.md and the manifest already carry it; the quality modal gains the texture row with its three sub-scores and the worst-tail evidence (the educator SEES what "templated" means in their own package).
- **Bar:** chip renders both numbers within the 12px/badge token law; no change to gating (texture stays weight 0 until the voice pass earns its flip); drift gate untouched.

### B3. A calmer crown

The title row currently stacks: title · Quality chip · "15 lessons · gpt-5.4-mini" · "9 compiled" · "4 auto-fixed" · Autosaved chip. Two of those are receipts, not status:

- "9 compiled" and "auto-fixed" chips move into the digest/receipt (they're already there) — the crown keeps title, Seal, lessons·model, autosave.
- The stray utility row between ribbon and tab bar (the lone ⇄ control at ~y370 in every screenshot) folds into the tab bar's right edge — that's a full row of vertical rhythm returned.
- **Bar:** crown carries ≤4 elements + the CTA cluster; the utility row is gone; tokens/dark scans green.
