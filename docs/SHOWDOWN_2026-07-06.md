**Readings, honestly:**

  interchangeable with OpenAI's workhorse mini tier *for this task under
  these gates*, at $0 and no network.

  kernels). But ds's solver-rejection rate swings hard between runs (it lost
  5 to the solver in the A1 run-1 measurement) — its margin is real on dense,
  variable elsewhere.
- `rhyme-scheme-and-internal-rhyme` is E2B's stable blind spot: 0/3 in five
  consecutive runs, plain and retry. ds solves it. Per-kernel routing (send
  known-dense kernels to a paid author) remains the org-chart answer.
- E2B is 3.4× faster than ds per kernel; mini is fastest (3.9s) but not $0.
