# v0.20.08 questions from the teacher's material

Date: 2026-09-22. Follows the live Scion quality test of v0.20.07, where 0 of 12 pre-registered course descriptions produced a usable lesson: the material a teacher wrote into the description (a poem, a dialogue, a data set, dated facts) never reached the quiz, and the compiler fell back to "Records A–D" template items.

## What changed

| Area             | v0.20.07                                                                              | v0.20.08                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Reading material | Only an English `Source facts:` label (2 of 12 test descriptions)                     | Quoted lines and poems, titled Chinese texts (《静夜思》：…), dialogues, English and Chinese fact labels, data and formulas (12 of 12). Requirement sentences such as "Session 3 must prove…" are not material      |
| Where it appears | Nowhere                                                                               | Verbatim card in the lesson plan, quiz, study guide and assignment, and in DOCX export                                                                                                                              |
| Quiz questions   | Deterministic templates ("Use Records A-D…")                                          | Scion writes one question per call from the material; each draft is checked (language, grounding, pipeline words, repeats, letter-only keys, two defensible options) and retried once                               |
| Calculations     | Model or template                                                                     | Computed in code: data-set statistics, limiting reagent and yield, uniform acceleration, single-gene crosses. Scion then writes conceptual questions only; model-keyed numbers the app did not compute are rejected |
| Fallback         | Template                                                                              | Questions built from the material: fill in a line, compare two statements, what a statement can and cannot show, translate a line                                                                                   |
| Chinese briefs   | "1节课，45分钟，每节4道题" read as Auto / Auto / 6; some courses generated in English | Course shape read from Chinese (including 一节课，四十五分钟); prompts ask for Chinese output                                                                                                                       |
| Wording          | Objectives such as "Distinguish admitted evidence … and bound the … conclusion"       | Plain objectives; a runtime guard rewrites pipeline terms in every compiled material                                                                                                                                |

Lessons with a reviewed teaching task (v0.20.05) keep their checked questions. The per-question calls are reserved in the run's call plan.

## Live result (browser-local Scion, Gemma 4 E2B)

Twelve pre-registered descriptions (`benchmarks/scion-live/briefs.json`, unchanged since v0.20.07), scored with `scripts/benchmarks/scionLiveScore.mjs` on what students answer, not on the quoted material card. Full record: `benchmarks/scion-live/results-v0.20.08.json`.

| Family                    | v0.20.07 usable | v0.20.08 automated | v0.20.08 read by hand                                 |
| ------------------------- | --------------- | ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Calculation (Q1–Q4)       | 0/4             | 4/4                | 4 clean, every key correct                            |
| Chronology (T1–T4)        | 0/4             | 3/4                | 1 clean, 1 after one fix, 2 not usable (both Chinese) |
| Text and argument (A1–A4) | 0/4             | 3/4                | 2 clean, 1 after one fix, 1 not usable (Spanish)      |

Each course took 44–85 s after the model was cached. The remaining failures are the model's source reasoning (reading one teacher as two, calling a larger count more reliable, treating a correlation as proof) and beginner-language questions. Review answer keys before teaching.
