# V0.17.01 Output Quality Audit

## Goal

Make generated results more honest when source evidence or model output is
incomplete. V0.17.01 prefers a visible gap and targeted retry to unsupported
source wiring or polished generic fallback content.

## Roundtable Review

Roundtable v0.0.0.26 reviewed the EduTool codebase in a follow-up two-round,
six-turn session after the earlier design discussion. The meeting was steered
to inspect implementation and tests rather than infer behavior from product
documentation. Its final consensus blocked the release on four concrete gaps:

- the FAQ fallback consumer was dead while synthetic FAQ padding remained;
- CourseIR could emit a repair receipt when no source reference changed;
- quiz retry used a literal five instead of the configured target; and
- underfilled-quiz telemetry disappeared when no mutation occurred.

The team also narrowed the source-locality claim, identified assessment-wide
rubric source-union inflation, required document-invariant semantic-skeleton
masking, and required exact shipped untuned figures to be evidence-bound.

### Six-round package-trust continuation

A later six-round, code-only Roundtable audit examined the V0.17.01 trust and
output-quality diff. Reviewers reproduced a source-free export where every atom
resolved to the synthetic `SL1` review row: structural coverage reported 8/8
while no trusted concept-linked evidence existed. They also found that invalid
fallback CourseIR could be projected into the exported graph, the frozen
untuned receipt was not enforced in CI, the 30-hit texture receipt lacked
20/10 stage attribution in its regression test, and a quality-policy exemption
depended on human prose.

The release closes those findings as follows:

- CourseIR now reports structural reference wiring and trusted evidence
  coverage separately.
- Final trust evidence fails closed unless both the source-ledger summary and
  per-reference coverage prove trusted concept-linked rows. Legacy manifests
  without trusted per-reference totals receive zero trusted coverage.
- Automated readiness and the pipeline judgment consume the same trusted
  per-reference totals. Structural 8/8 wiring to review rows cannot become
  grounding merely because one unrelated trusted row exists.
- Invalid fallback CourseIR remains visible as review proof but is never
  projected as the export graph.
- CI runs the frozen untuned receipt verifier, and the release regression binds
  its 30 hits to 20 blueprint-stage plus 10 deliverable-stage hits.
- Discipline-density findings carry the stable
  `discipline-term-density-low` code through the grader accumulator. A graded
  package regression proves the live finding reaches policy evaluation. The
  prose matcher remains only for persisted findings from grader V1.11.2 and
  earlier.
- `SOURCE_REPORT.md` labels ordinary sourceRef counts as structural wiring and
  reports trusted per-reference coverage separately. A legacy or bridged
  package without exact trusted atom links says that proof is unavailable.

The team did not reach evidence for a proposed broad lens matrix or
delta-repair change, so neither entered the release.

A focused post-change Roundtable review found the readiness/judgment consumer
gap and the dropped finding code above. It also proposed treating the legacy
source bridge as trusted atom coverage. That proposal was rejected in
cross-examination: the bridge replaces the ledger but does not rewrite or prove
the atom references, so fail-closed trusted coverage is the correct result.

A final exact-snapshot review reproduced one further fail-open path: when the
trusted coverage block was absent, automated readiness could fall back to a
prose-derived enrichment percentage. That percentage describes tagged output
bytes, not atom-to-source proof. V0.17.01 removes the fallback, requires trusted
per-reference totals for the grounding portion of readiness, and adds a bridge
regression proving that even a claimed 100% prose grounding fraction earns zero
grounding credit when trusted proof is unavailable. The stricter behavior also
refreshes the still-unreleased V34 17-file implementation receipt.

The exact-commit follow-up also caught the executable readiness benchmark still
feeding its positive control through structural coverage and expecting the
legacy one-source package to remain in the strong band. The benchmark now
supplies explicit trusted totals for its positive control, expects legacy
evidence to remain bounded, freezes exact 26/59/68 scores as well as their
bands, rejects any case without a finite exact score, hash-binds the canonical
fixture, and runs in CI.

## Independent Audit

The source-fallback finding was valid but the discussion overstated its score
effect. Arbitrary references could make source-coverage manifests look complete
and could affect readiness when trusted concept-linked evidence was also
present. They did not universally produce a fixed readiness-score promotion.
The release therefore claims a coverage-integrity repair, not a universal score
change.

Accepted:

- `fallbackSourceRefsForLesson` and assessment repair must use only validated,
  locally linked references.
- Synthesized concepts must not receive `SL1` when no supporting source exists.
- Unusable FAQ generation must remain failed and retryable; no synthetic
  fallback artifact or missing-question padding is justified.
- Quiz retry and readiness must use the configured target, while underfill
  remains inspectable without being called a repair.
- Criterion repair must prefer criterion-linked outcomes and lessons rather
  than the assessment-wide source union.
- The repetition grader must mask every lesson title consistently in every
  compared document with token-safe boundaries.
- A disjoint untuned panel is useful report-only evidence of generalization
  limits when exact figures are bound to a canonical receipt.

Rejected or deferred:

- Post-export reference stripping was rejected. It would hide an upstream
  ownership defect and could remove legitimate provenance after compilation.
- New distractor rules were not added because the observed padding defect
  generated short-answer questions, not distractors.
- Dynamic lens creation and a larger hand-authored lens table were deferred.
  This release first establishes an untuned measurement lane.
- No claim is made that a present reference proves source correctness, factual
  accuracy, pedagogical effectiveness, accessibility, instructor approval, or
  classroom outcomes.

## Lane

### Evidence integrity

CourseIR repair now leaves unsupported atom references empty, does not invent a
source ledger, and emits `added-atom-source-refs` only when at least one atom
actually changes. Two-lesson adversarial fixtures prove that evidence for one
lesson cannot manufacture coverage for another and that each rubric criterion
recovers only from its linked outcomes and lessons. A second repair pass is
idempotent.

### Fail-closed learner artifacts

Unusable Course FAQ generation stays failed and retryable. CourseMapper no
longer builds a deterministic FAQ fallback or pads underfilled lessons with
invented questions. Quiz generation removes underfilled lessons for retry using
the configured target; readiness uses the same target. Deterministic repair
returns quiz and FAQ count gaps as observations even when it makes no mutation.

### Semantic repetition and untuned measurement

Deep grader V1.11.3 retains document-invariant, token-safe lesson-title masking
and adds a stable discipline-density policy code. Short titles such as SQL,
Git, RNA, and C++ remain boundary-safe without changing larger words such as
`database`. The new V34 held-out benchmark freezes this changed transitive
grader implementation as an executable boundary and inherits no V33 score,
adapter win, or promotion claim.

The 12-course untuned panel is disjoint from the frozen thin panel and records
content-fallback telemetry through the same SSR compiler runtime used by the
texture audit. The release command verifies the complete canonical result
against `evaluation/cross-package-texture/untuned-v0.17.01-receipt.json`.

The first V0.17.01 characterization produced:

- 12 packages and 468 path-free input-mask clusters;
- 7.45% support burden;
- 11.91% reader exposure;
- 8.28% cross-package excess;
- 30 `lens-default` hits across 10 packages; and
- zero unclassified extracted paths.

These receipt-bound values describe the current unrecognized-course boundary.
They are not a before/after quality win or a ratchet.

## Release Boundary

V0.17.01 changes deterministic repair, fallback validation, semantic repetition
measurement, and report-only untuned auditing. It does not change Gemma weights,
activate the optional adapter, certify sources, or establish provider,
instructor, accessibility, learner, or classroom superiority.
