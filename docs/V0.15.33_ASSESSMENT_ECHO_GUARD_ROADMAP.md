2. Syllabus surfaces repeated assessment-title chains such as

The digest also stayed honest about broader quality caveats:

## Goal

Let AI Governance packages export when they legitimately use model-card/model
documentation language, and remove deterministic numbered assessment echoes
from syllabus surfaces without weakening citation, genome, texture, or notebook
asset checks.

## Lane

Narrow compiler/export-policy cleanup. This release fixes the blocked export
and visible syllabus corruption exposed by the audit; it does not claim the
separate slide-deck texture warning or zero-genome-link caveat is solved.

## Fix Plan

1. Normalize assessment labels earlier in the compiler.
   - Strip deterministic `Title: 1. Title` echoes when registry titles become
     student artifacts, assessment anchors, and syllabus rows.
   - Preserve the meaningful assessment identity and percentage.

2. Sanitize embedded weight evidence.
   - Weight provenance can carry source text into exported surfaces. It should
     not preserve raw numbered-title echoes even when the evidence snippet is a
     clipped source fragment.

3. Split the lab-assets validator.
   - Keep hard blockers for notebook/Jupyter/IPYNB/starter-notebook leakage in
     non-data-science packages.
   - Allow model-card language when the course context is explicitly AI
     governance, model documentation, algorithmic accountability, AI risk
     management, data ethics, or public-sector AI procurement.

4. Add focused regressions.
   - Compiler regression: the Policy Memo syllabus should not contain
     `Lesson 4 concept transfer: Policy memo methods (25%): 1. Lesson 4...`.
   - Validator regression: AI Governance model-card language should pass while
     notebook assets remain blocked for non-data-science courses.

## Non-Goals

- Do not weaken source-ledger, genome, or texture grading.
- Do not claim a live post-fix 100/100 package until a deployed audit proves it.
- Do not suppress the slide-deck repeated-phrase warning exposed by the audit
  without a separate artifact-backed fix.
- Do not allow Jupyter, IPYNB, or starter-notebook leakage for ordinary
  non-data-science courses.

## Release Boundary

Ship only if the focused compiler and pedagogical-validator regressions,
release-history audit, format, lint, full test suite, gold smoke, build, bundle
check, and browser verification pass locally. After push, Fast verification and
Pages deploy must also complete before the release is claimed.
