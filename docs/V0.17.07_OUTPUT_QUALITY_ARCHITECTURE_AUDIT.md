# V0.17.07 Output Quality Architecture Audit

Date: 2026-07-31

Status: **not a classroom-quality checkpoint**

Decision owner: Codex, after a six-round code-and-artifact Roundtable review

## Goal

Make EDUTOOL v0.17.07 tell the truth about what its automated evidence can and cannot establish, then freeze further artifact expansion until a small external instructor experiment can separate model, retrieval, and projection effects.

## Lane

Engineering integrity: package-proof binding, provenance preservation, semantic-substitution removal, adversarial measurement, and binding independent-evidence gates. This lane improves the system and its output diagnostics without claiming that automation has certified teaching quality.

## Release Boundary

V0.17.07 may ship verified software changes and bounded formatting improvements. It may not be called a classroom-quality checkpoint, a factual certification, an instructor-approved package, or proof that Gemma versus the compiler is the binding bottleneck. That boundary remains closed until the strict paired benchmark and a real external instructor packet are complete.

## Executive finding

EDUTOOL's persistent output-quality ceiling is not explained by one weak model. Gemma 4 E2B has a demonstrated capability gap, but the larger and more immediate failure is the system around it:

1. a small set of model/source atoms is expanded by a large deterministic compiler into many artifact families;
2. rejected or missing knowledge falls into an always-available template lane instead of stopping authoring;
3. internal graders measure package conformance and lexical defects more reliably than domain truth or teachability; and
4. the external instructor gate that could falsify those proxies was deliberately made optional in v0.8.2.

The result is a closed optimization loop. The compiler creates the prose, the grader rewards the compiler's detectable properties, and releases can pass without one retained external instructor scorecard or edit history.

## Evidence that separates the causes

### The model ceiling is real

The retained paired-order lesson-kernel pilot under `evaluation/scion-adapters/evidence/lesson-kernel-pilot-v0.16.56/current/judge/paired-order-result.json` preferred the reference model in all three cases. Gemma outputs included wrong answer keys, answer/explanation contradictions, duplicated choices, unsupported claims, and a reversed capacitance relationship.

This proves that a stronger model can improve the atoms. It does **not** prove that swapping the model will repair the exported package.

### The compiler is the dominant visible author

`src/hooks/useDeliverables.js` explicitly describes one payload producing quiz, slides, study guide, discussion, and assignment content. `src/lib/courseBlueprintCompiler.js` then projects the shared blueprint into nine deliverable families and contains hundreds of rotating sentence-template sites.

This architecture amplifies one weak atom into many polished-looking surfaces. It also encourages local template repairs that remove detectable defects without adding disciplinary knowledge. During this audit, a proposed repeated-misconception repair did exactly that; it was removed rather than shipped.

### The quality score is a conformance score

The retained v0.17.06 package reported 99/A package conformance while automated readiness was 54, evidence grounding was 35, and no lesson kernel was admitted. That is not a contradiction in the current schema: the 99/A score measures encoded package defects, not teaching quality.

The previous readiness implementation was also partly self-referential. It parsed phrases such as `knowledge kernels admitted 8/8` from pipeline prose and awarded source-reference wiring credit. Rewording a status sentence could therefore change the score while leaving every exported lesson byte unchanged.

The supposedly stronger entailment receipt is not a semantic solution. On the live research path, the extracted claim and its anchor quote are commonly the same string, so the check proves extraction integrity. The lexical fallback can accept relation reversals and role swaps while rejecting faithful paraphrases. It must not gate downstream instructional claims.

### The independent objective was disconnected

`ROADMAP.md` records the v0.8.2 decision to remove external expert audit as a release dependency. `scripts/deepProofQualityGate.mjs` then treated the independent instructor benchmark as non-blocking, ran the internal expert-style audit, and treated proof-packet generation as part of the gate despite its optional label.

The strict command already exposes the truth:

- `npm run audit:expert:preflight` fails;
- proof status is `internal-provisional-only`;
- external proof readiness is 1/12;
- there are zero external fixtures, scorecards, edit histories, and proof-eligible samples; and
- required 5-, 8-, and 14-lesson proof scopes are missing.

This is the upstream cause of the treadmill. Model weakness, compiler fan-out, fallback prose, and grader coupling are mechanisms. Optional external evidence is why the system could not learn which mechanism dominated.

## Changes made during this audit

1. Package quality receipts are bound to the exact export scope, grader version, and texture version, so a full-course or stale receipt cannot authorize a different ZIP.
2. Reader-visible repetition, long identity leakage, generic DOCX headers, clinical short-package false positives, and several copy defects now have regression coverage.
3. Source extraction receipts retain their per-claim source ID, locator, quotation, and check result through the source ledger.
4. Automated readiness no longer parses pipeline prose or internal source-reference counts as evidence. Extraction receipts remain visible as traceability diagnostics but receive zero semantic-grounding credit and explicitly state that downstream claim support is false.
5. Semantic compiler substitution for repeated misconceptions/corrections was removed. Repetition stays visible as a defect and regeneration signal.
6. An honest missing-definition notice remains a scored unresolved finding; replacing nonsense with a warning no longer makes the audit empty.
7. The deep proof gate now makes the independent instructor benchmark and external instructor preflight blocking. The proof-packet builder is genuinely advisory.
8. The grounded Algi/Scion/hybrid protocol now runs in strict mode from the ordinary package command and from Deep Proof. Missing paired evidence exits nonzero instead of being recorded as proof.
9. Rendered-deck QA fixed four deterministic projection defects without inventing disciplinary content: acronym lowercasing, chopped long assessment labels, one-bullet evidence slides, and a concept-map word split. A LibreOffice-specific disappearing-checkmark defect now has a renderer-stable implementation and regression test.

These changes improve honesty, traceability, formatting, and failure visibility. They do not constitute independent evidence that the lessons are good enough to teach unchanged.

## Fresh-package result after removing semantic laundering

The same four-lesson digital-accessibility fixture was regenerated through the real ZIP exporter after the audit changes. The result moved in the direction an honest system should:

| Signal                    | Before | V0.17.07 audited build |
| ------------------------- | -----: | ---------------------: |
| Package conformance       |   99/A |                   89/B |
| Automated readiness       | 54/100 |                 47/100 |
| Evidence-grounding credit | 35/100 |                  0/100 |
| P1 findings               |      0 |                      1 |

The P1 is not a new regression. It is an authored correction shell repeated across the fixture—`requires its stated condition, applicable scope, and inspectable interface evidence`—that the compiler previously could have cosmetically varied. V0.17.07 leaves it visible and blocks a high-quality claim. This is a more useful result than the previous A because it identifies the upstream authoring defect instead of rewarding the package for hiding it.

Representative DOCX and PPTX artifacts were rendered, visually inspected, and overflow-tested. The documents remain structurally clean; the first lesson deck now keeps `Conformance` unbroken, retains a substantive three-bullet evidence slide, preserves `WCAG` casing, uses the complete reader-facing `Week 1 brief` label, and renders all three checklist markers consistently. The presentation overflow check reports no content outside the slide canvas.

## The next falsifiable experiment already exists

The repository already freezes a three-arm, five-domain protocol in `evaluation/algi/algi-scion-hybrid-benchmark-v1.json`:

- `algi`: evidence retrieval plus deterministic compilation with zero model inference;
- `scion`: the current Scion route through the same compiler; and
- `hybrid`: an admitted evidence ledger followed by one grounded-authoring call.

Its ordinary package command previously supplied neither evidence nor strict mode. The audit returned `status: ready`, `promotionEligible: false`, and exit code zero, while release contracts counted that invocation as proof. V0.17.07 changes the normal command to strict mode and adds it to Deep Proof, so an unexecuted protocol is now a release failure.

Do not populate the existing `quality` and `blindWinner` fields from EDUTOOL's internal grader; that would move the proxy loop one level outward. Complete the protocol with one lesson's study guide as a vertical slice with:

- one instructor-curated source packet;
- five obligations fixed before generation: definition, relationship, worked example, misconception/correction, and practice rationale;
- a claim ledger that retains source ID, locator, quotation, authored claim, and final rendered location;
- model-authored prose with an independent critic and at most three revision attempts;
- a compiler that may format or reject, but may not create or repair semantic prose; and
- two blinded domain instructors recording factual defects, edit minutes, and whether each obligation was accepted unchanged.

Retain the zero-inference Algi arm to test whether Scion currently adds reader-visible value. Add a same-anchor frontier-model hybrid comparison when the three-arm result cannot separate pipeline loss from model capacity:

|                | Current projection | Validator-only claim ledger |
| -------------- | ------------------ | --------------------------- |
| Gemma 4 E2B    | control            | architecture intervention   |
| Frontier model | model intervention | combined intervention       |

Primary metric: **obligation-weighted unchanged instructor acceptance**. A quotation may establish support but cannot fulfill a teaching obligation. An omitted obligation remains a failure, preventing deletion gaming.

Decision rule:

- current model contribution is negligible if the zero-inference Algi arm ties or beats current Scion under blind review;
- architecture is dominant if the validator-only effect exceeds the model effect and Gemma improves materially;
- model capacity is dominant if the frontier model substantially beats Gemma under the same anchors and validator-only pipeline; and
- retrieval is dominant if both models succeed on curated sources but fail when normal source acquisition is restored.

## Release decision

Do not call v0.17.07 a classroom-quality checkpoint until at least the small external experiment above is complete. Internal tests may establish software correctness and package conformance. They cannot certify factual accuracy, teachability, accessibility in use, or instructor acceptance.
