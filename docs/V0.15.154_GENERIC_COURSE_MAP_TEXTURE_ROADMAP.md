## Scope

V0.15.154 keeps the release narrow:

1. Replace the generic weekly assessment `Quick evidence check` family with
   varied application, transfer, exit-note, and short-response prompts.
2. Replace generic `Review assigned materials` async tasks with source-note,
   annotation, sample-response, and question-drafting tasks.
3. Replace broad LMS/tooling and old supporting-resource copy with
   instructor-provided resource, activity-prompt, reference-note, and
   feedback-guide language.
4. Replace repeated evidence-standard review notes with visible-product,
   source-detail, observable-response, and reusable-assessment evidence checks.
5. Preserve domain-specific profiles such as CS/Python, UX, Project
   Management, and History.
