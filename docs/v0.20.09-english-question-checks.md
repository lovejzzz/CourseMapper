# v0.20.09 English question checks

Date: 2026-09-22. Scope: English courses only; Scion's base model is unchanged (Gemma 4 E2B).

## What the live English runs of v0.20.08 still got wrong

- A line given to the wrong speaker (French: Paul's "J'habite à Lyon" keyed as Marie's; Spanish: Luis's "Soy de México" put in Ana's mouth).
- A figure given to the wrong source (the union's 1,200 keyed as the owners' newspaper's), and "the larger count is more reliable".
- Four look-up questions and no reasoning, or three questions on the same figure.
- No calculation for price and quantity data.

## What changed

| Area               | v0.20.09                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Who said what      | Speakers are read from their own introductions; sourced facts are split into source and claim. Drafts that move a line or figure to someone else are rejected, and the prompt restates the material as "who says what". |
| Reasoning          | Drafts that judge reliability by the size of a number, treat a correlation as proof in a cause-and-effect lesson, or give away their own answer are rejected.                                                           |
| Question mix       | At most one look-up question per short quiz; questions testing the same point are dropped; the quiz is always filled to the requested count.                                                                            |
| Built-in questions | Source–figure pairing; why sources give different figures; the confounding question; percentage change and revenue; four exact questions for motion, reactions and data sets (no model call).                           |
