# Scion Truth Gate checkpoint — v0.17.13

## Goal

Make Scion a better learner without hard-coding a course: let it ask bounded questions, accept source-authorized teaching, retain only strict improvements, and prevent any candidate from entering training merely because Scion or its producer says it is good.

## Decision

Freeze **v0.17.13 — Truth Before Training** as an admission-system checkpoint.

The six-seed pilot passed the executable Truth Gate: two independent Roundtable participants reviewed six frozen atoms across computer science, geology, and music theory; all 12 signed receipts were valid; all six atoms were admitted; and the audit reported zero issues.

This is not a Scion quality checkpoint. No Scion inference ran, no model score was produced, no weights changed, and neither training nor production promotion is authorized.

## Why the architecture needed to change

The long-running quality problem was not only model capacity. The prior loop mixed generation, teaching, judging, and promotion too freely:

1. rejected answers could contaminate repair prompts;
2. whole-atom tasks hid the field that actually failed;
3. lexical similarity could masquerade as pedagogy;
4. source indexes could survive while the underlying claim drifted;
5. self-authored or incomplete review metadata could be mistaken for independent approval;
6. invalid gold seeds could turn a carefully preregistered experiment into a false measurement.

The remedy is separation of authority: the learner may ask and propose; a bounded teacher may advise; deterministic gates may diagnose; but only source-bound, independently signed, executable review receipts may admit a complete seed.

## Roundtable consultation

The review process itself was iterative and materially changed the implementation.

- The first audit found fail-open verdict handling, arbitrary reviewer metadata, incomplete authority binding, exact-only overlap checks, and discovery readiness that was not conjoined with review readiness.
- The next audit rejected source packets whose excerpts were too narrow, so evidence became bounded multi-capture packets.
- The first Git-frozen packet was rejected because examples exceeded their captures and the future holdout lacked full packet binding and prior-source exclusion.
- The next packet admitted four of six atoms and rejected tuple hashability and deceptive motion for claims broader than the captures.
- Commit `8afc2ebc184a5a8c877d1cf47892c55f58fc26e0` narrowed those two atoms and froze the final packet.
- The final sealed review admitted all six atoms. Codex and Antigravity supplied distinct signed messages; Claude was unavailable and Roundtable continued without requiring another login.

The honest rejections are part of the proof. Earlier packets did not become passing evidence after the fact.

## Lane 1 — training admission

- Browser producers cannot self-authorize semantic preference rows.
- Same-model repairs remain diagnostic and training-ineligible.
- Preference admission requires explicit historical opt-in for legacy rows.
- Flywheel candidates must re-enter through the current gate rather than inheriting old eligibility.

## Lane 2 — learner and teacher

- Scion asks course-neutral questions only after its local retry budget is exhausted.
- Questions expose bounded issue families and allowed actions, not hidden answers.
- Roundtable teaching remains source-authorized, diagnostic, and training-ineligible by default.
- The retention selector keeps a valid control, accepts a teacher rescue only when it strictly passes, and quarantines uncertainty.

## Lane 3 — executable Truth Gate

- Every seed binds its complete atom, bounded source captures, canonical source identity, and packet identity.
- Exact and conservative semantic overlap checks reject reused projects, prompts, URLs, source IDs, evidence hashes, and packet hashes.
- Each Roundtable participant message is Ed25519-signed by a bridge key whose fingerprint was registered before review.
- Verdicts, blindness flags, reasons, reviewer identity, message identity, and raw-message hashes are reconstructed from verified signed bodies rather than trusted from submitted receipt fields.
- Missing verdicts, tampering, self-signed untrusted keys, duplicate reviewers, duplicate messages, and source overlap fail closed.
- Pilot readiness requires both discovery sufficiency and a valid receipt gate.

## Full holdout remains blocked

The full source-disjoint holdout still has zero eligible new cases and a deficit of four in each required domain. It needs 12 new source-disjoint atoms, two bound independent reviews per atom, full prior-source exclusion, and a frozen packet before any inference or scoring.

The failed v0.17.12 holdout remains negative evidence. Its invalid gold labels are not rewritten as a quality win.

## Verification

- Truth Gate pilot: passed; 6/6 admitted, 12/12 receipts valid, 0 issues.
- Full holdout preflight: correctly blocked; deficits 4/4/4.
- Truth Gate unit suite covers empty-verdict, signature-tamper, untrusted-key, duplicate-review, and semantic-overlap attacks.
- Full unit suite: 520 files passed, 6,702 tests passed, 16 files and 162 tests skipped.
- Cross-package texture audit remains within its frozen receipt.
- Lint, formatting, production build, bundle budgets, release history, and `git diff --check` passed.

## Release Boundary

This release proves that one frozen six-seed pilot can be admitted through a source-bound, Git-bound, cryptographically attested dual-review gate.

It does **not** prove learned ability, model-quality improvement, classroom readiness, generalization, adapter quality, training eligibility, production eligibility, or deployment. Those claims require a new 12-case source-disjoint holdout and separate promotion evidence.
