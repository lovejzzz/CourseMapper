# v0.16.2 Roadmap — Evidence to Decision

**Status:** implemented and locally verified

**Target:** v0.16.2
**Release Boundary:** compiler-contract release; no independent-teachability claim

## Goal

Make every accepted lesson scenario a usable evidence-to-decision packet, and make every quality surface measure that same contract. A scenario is ready only when it gives students a concrete context, an actionable decision or problem, inspectable evidence, a real tension or constraint, and specific materials.

v0.16.2 does not claim that Scion is generally better than a reference model. It closes a measured product gap and repairs the ruler used to measure it.

## Evidence at the start

The July 10 User Experience Design Studio pair reported:

- applied multiple-choice reasoning: Scion 59.1%, reference 59.1%;
- supported multiple-choice inference: 100% for both;
- contrastive rationales: Scion 100%, reference 9.1%;
- decision-ready scenarios: Scion 54.5%, reference 66.7%;
- cue-free and claim-evidence-boundary short answers: Scion 100%, reference 0%.

Inspection found two defects in the scenario metric:

1. it searched only `scenario.setup`, even though the kernel contract stores inspectable evidence in `scenario.materials`; and
2. it divided by scenarios present, not lessons, so a missing scenario did not lower the decision-ready share.

The latest Scion run had 11 scenarios for 12 lessons. Several scenarios marked weak already contained an explicit decision plus concrete logs, data, transcripts, prototypes, or policy details in the materials field. Lesson 8 had no accepted scenario and is the real coverage failure.

## Lane A — One scenario contract

Create one shared, explainable contract used by authoring, admission, deterministic projection, and evaluation.

The contract reports five independent checks:

1. **Context:** at least 20 words of concrete setup.
2. **Decision:** an explicit choice/recommendation/next step, or an actionable problem that requires resolution.
3. **Evidence packet:** named observations, results, records, quotes, data, artifacts, or multiple inspectable materials across setup and materials.
4. **Tension:** alternatives, constraints, conflict, failure, uncertainty, or a bounded decision.
5. **Materials:** a specific inspectable packet, not “scenario evidence” or “course materials.”

The analyzer must be null-safe and return issue codes, not only a boolean, so prompt tests, admission logs, and contrast reports can explain failures.

## Lane B — Grounded zero-call fallback

When a model scenario is absent or fails admission, derive one without another provider call and without inventing disciplinary claims.

The fallback may reuse only already-admitted kernel atoms:

- one concrete key-term example;
- its documented misconception and correction; and
- one admitted fact related to that term.

It must frame those atoms as competing interpretations, ask for a bounded decision, name the evidence packet, and record `derived-kernel-fallback` provenance. It must never fabricate a person, institution, statistic, quotation, policy, or citation.

The authored scenario remains preferred whenever it passes the shared contract.

## Lane C — Projection and evaluation integrity

- Short-answer prompts and model answers consume the resolved authored-or-derived scenario, not the pre-recovery nullable field.
- The saved kernel retains scenario provenance so audits can distinguish authored quality from compiler coverage.
- `decisionReadyScenarios` and `concreteScenarioMaterials` use all lessons as their denominator.
- Add `scenarioCoverage` as a separate paired diagnostic dimension.
- The contrast report lists the first failing contract reasons and counts derived fallbacks.
- Preserve the existing claim boundary: one saved course pair is directional evidence, not model-superiority proof.

## Lane D — Public and cloud authoring alignment

Both the standard kernel prompt and Scion Draft prompt must ask for the same five-part contract. Prompt examples must put evidence in both setup and materials deliberately, and must not imply that a number or quote is mandatory in every discipline.

## Verification bars

All bars are required before pushing v0.16.2 to `main`:

### Deterministic

- Scenario-contract unit matrix covers strong, missing, generic-material, evidence-only, decision-only, and cross-discipline cases.
- Kernel parse/projection tests prove authored scenarios are preserved and rejected/missing scenarios receive a grounded fallback with provenance.
- Contrast tests prove missing lessons lower coverage/readiness and null input cannot crash the audit.
- Full tests, lint, release-history audit, production build, and bundle budgets pass.
- Pull-request and main evaluation profiles pass at the compiler-contract level.

### Real public Scion run

On a fresh 12-lesson User Experience Design Studio generation:

- package grade at least 99/A;
- zero P0, P1, or P2 findings;
- 12/12 accepted or grounded scenarios;
- at least 90% decision-ready scenarios using all lessons as denominator;
- applied multiple-choice share at least 55%;
- the contrast audit evaluates these as explicit release bars and exits nonzero in strict mode;
- supported-inference share 100%;
- contrastive-rationale share at least 90%;
- cue-free and claim-evidence-boundary short answers 100%; and
- representative quiz DOCX files pass rendered visual inspection.

If public generation is unavailable, rate-limited, or does not meet these bars, v0.16.2 does not push to `main` on deterministic proof alone.

## Release truth

The v0.16.2 changelog and release contract may claim:

- one shared evidence-to-decision scenario contract;
- grounded zero-call scenario recovery;
- denominator-correct paired diagnostics;
- the exact fresh browser and rendered-output evidence that was retained.

They may not claim independent instructor validation, general Scion superiority, or production proof until the independent benchmark and production-canary policy pass.
