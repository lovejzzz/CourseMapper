# CourseMapper instructor review — one page

Thank you for looking at this. The ZIP beside this guide is a complete course
package CourseMapper generated **without human editing**: syllabus, lesson
plans, slide decks, quizzes, assignments, rubrics, discussions, study guides,
and a course FAQ, compiled around openly licensed source material (citations
appear where content is source-anchored).

We don't need a polite review — we need to know where it breaks. Our internal
graders already check structure and consistency; **what no machine can tell us
is whether a working instructor would actually teach from it.**

## The three questions that matter

1. **Would you teach Week 3 as-is?** Open the Week 3 lesson plan, deck, and
   quiz. If you would not walk into class with them, what is the FIRST thing
   you would change?
2. **What would your students notice is missing?** Not what a checklist finds —
   what the people in the room would feel the absence of.
3. **Where does it stop sounding like a teacher?** Mark the first place the
   materials read like they were assembled rather than authored. Page and line
   if you can.

## How to mark it up

Any form works: margin notes on a printout, comments in the files, or three
paragraphs in an email. Specific beats complete — one concrete "this exercise
would not survive contact with my 9am section, because…" is worth more than a
general impression.

## What happens with your notes

Your verdict becomes the north star for the next development cycle — the
product's quality loop is built around exactly this kind of judgment (an LLM
judge currently caps our materials at "usable with revision"; you are the
instrument above that ceiling).
