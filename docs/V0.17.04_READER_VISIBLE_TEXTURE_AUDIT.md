# V0.17.04 Reader-Visible Texture Audit

## Goal

Make a proven reader-visible repetition defect affect deterministic package
conformance and repair its dominant compiler owner without broadening the rule
beyond the retained evidence.

## Lane

Deterministic compiler realization, visible-unit texture measurement, deep
quality grading, and release-evidence truthfulness.

## Release Boundary

The score-bearing policy covers lesson-plan skeleton repetition only. It does
not score other artifact families, block downloads, or make a human-quality
claim.

## Decision

V0.17.04 adds a score-bearing regression guard without pretending every kind of
repetition has the same instructional meaning. The visible-unit metric already
counted exact and slot-masked paragraph skeletons by artifact family, but those
counts could not affect conformance. Deep grader V1.11.5 now applies one narrow,
versioned policy to lesson plans. Current retained output sits below the guard;
this is not a claim that the broader visible-repetition gap is solved.

## Artifact evidence

The local retained `trellis/runs/e0-golden/report.json` records a 90 texture
score and no texture finding while its visible-unit receipt reports 51.43%
global skeleton extra duplicates. Its lesson-plan family is materially lower at
8.16%. That run directory is intentionally ignored and is a development sanity
check, not a release-contract anchor.

The earlier two-package settlement in
`docs/QUALITY_MEASUREMENT_SETTLEMENT_2026-07-24.md` tested alternate masking
specifications and found lesson plans ranked first in all eight comparisons.
Under the settled specification B, the two known-bad lesson-plan rates were
17.0% and 16.4%. That evidence supports a lesson-plan boundary; it does not
support one global threshold for rubrics, policies, quizzes, or other families.

## Scoring contract

`visible-units.v1` evaluates `lessonPlans.skeleton.extraDuplicateRate` only when
at least 40 reader-visible units are eligible:

- below 15%: no finding and no penalty;
- 15% through 29.99%: P2 and 3 texture points;
- 30% or above: P1 and 8 texture points.

Under the grader's existing global severity policy, any P1 also caps package
conformance at 89. The Markdown receipt states that consequence beside the
texture-point effect; the release does not present the eight-point dimension
penalty as the entire package-level consequence.

The quality report seals the policy version, signal, family, eligible-unit
count, observed rate, threshold table, severity, penalty, and top repeated
cluster. All other families stay visible and score-neutral pending calibration.
Packages with some lesson-plan units but fewer than 40 receive a non-scoring
advisory so “not evaluated” cannot be mistaken for a passing evaluation.

## Compiler repair

The retained cross-package analysis identified the independent-work sentence
“Provide targeted feedback … and confirm readiness for submission” as a
dominant lesson-plan frame. Its single owner in `courseBlueprintCompiler.js`
now uses 36 deterministic lead-tail compositions grounded in lesson concept,
artifact, evidence, decision, limitation, and revision context.

## Claim boundary

This release improves deterministic variation and makes one encoded repetition
defect affect package conformance. It does not block downloads, change model
weights, validate facts, certify accessibility, replace instructor review, or
claim classroom effectiveness. Purpose-aware source admission remains a future
candidate that requires a fresh post-V0.17.03 output reproduction.
