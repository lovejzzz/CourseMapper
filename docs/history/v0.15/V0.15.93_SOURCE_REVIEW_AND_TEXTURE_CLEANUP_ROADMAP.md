# v0.15.93 Source Review and Texture Cleanup Roadmap

_Written June 28, 2026. Target app version: `0.15.93`._

## Goal

Use the fresh deployed `v0.15.92` User Experience Design Studio ZIP/log audit
to reduce the remaining non-clean quality debt without hiding it. The captured
package regraded at `96/A`: citations fell to `70/100` because ten
quarantined source candidates were still exported as instructor review caveats,
and texture stayed at `91/100` because lesson-plan and slide-deck compiler
phrases repeated across all 12 lessons.

Evidence folder:

`verification-output/edutool-audit-loop/20260628T074005Z-v01592-ux-fresh`

## Lane 1: Source Review Rows Stop Becoming Export Clutter

- Keep false-friend and metadata-only candidates out of trusted `sourceLedger`
  proof.
- When a source-finder topic already has trusted, licensed, concept-linked
  rows, do not export the rejected candidates as `sourceReviewRows`.
- Preserve review notes when no trusted source exists, because that is real
  citation debt.

## Lane 2: DOI-Backed UX/HCI Readings Become Trusted Proof

- Treat DOI-bearing, license-safe syllabus readings as Crossref-style proof
  when they are concept-linked.
- Recognize HCI and human-AI interaction as legitimate UX/design anchors.
- Do not promote readings with missing licenses, missing URLs/DOIs, or weak
  topic fit.

## Lane 3: Deterministic Texture Tails Vary Across Lessons

- Vary the lesson-plan modality check instead of stamping "not only topic
  recall" into every plan.
- Vary lesson-plan phase labels such as independent work, closure, and
  live-question discussion.
- Vary slide-deck visual descriptors and common-pitfall closing notes so
  repeated shingles do not dominate the texture evidence.

## Release Boundary

This release is a bounded product/compiler/export cleanup. It does not weaken
the source grader, does not hide real missing-source debt, and does not claim a
clean `100/100` provider result until a fresh deployed `v0.15.93` ZIP/log audit
proves the exported package has no citation caveats and improved texture.
