# v0.15.40 Source Relevance and Slide Polish Roadmap

_Written June 20, 2026. App version: `0.15.40`._

## Goal

Ship the smallest evidence-backed release that fixes the fresh Genetics and
Society v0.15.39 audit without weakening quality grading: generic background
sources must not become trusted genetics evidence, and long authored slide
bullets must not trip the truncated-bullet P1 gate because they lost sentence
punctuation.

## Lane

Source-finder retrieval relevance and slide-deck export polish. This lane stays
inside deterministic source filtering and compiler normalization. It does not
attempt the broader CurriculumV1 research compiler, source-license policy, or
provider architecture redesign.

## Release Boundary

In scope: source-finder discipline-anchor filtering for background providers,
final slide-bullet export punctuation, release manifest, contract, roadmap, and
focused regression coverage. Out of scope: resolving ambiguous OpenLibrary or
Crossref metadata licenses, changing quality thresholds, changing provider
cost strategy, or claiming that all CurriculumV1 atoms are fully source-grounded.

## Evidence

The 20260620T220413Z EduTool.dev audit ran against deployed v0.15.39
(`83bab01`). Fast verification and Pages deploy were green before the run. The
fresh Genetics and Society package exported successfully and downloaded as:

- `verification-output/edutool-audit-loop/20260620T220413Z/Genetics and Society - Course Materials (3).zip`

Local regrade reproduced the package report:

- quality: `98/100` (`A`)
- texture: `94/100`
- findings: `0 P0`, `1 P1`, `3 P2`
- export verification: passed

The P1 was deterministic slide-deck output in
`Slide Decks/Lesson 04 - Population genetics and ancestry claims - Slide Decks.pptx`:

- `Claims about group differences often overstate what genetic data can show`

The same deck contained the matching takeaway with a period, so the failure was
not a model hallucination; it was a final display/export normalization gap.

The source report also showed a source-finder relevance miss. Source row `sf2`
attached Wikipedia `Driving under the influence` to genetics concepts such as
environmental influence, trait variation, and nature/nurture. v0.15.37 blocked
generic academic metadata traps, but the anchor rule still let Wikipedia
background rows bypass the genetics source-text requirement.

## Fix

v0.15.40 adds a final slide-bullet export pass over every compiled slide. Long
authored bullets go through the same terminal-punctuation rule as passthrough
enrichment bullets. Short labels remain bare, and relationship-arrow concept-map
pairs remain exempt.

The source-finder discipline-anchor gate now applies to all retrieved providers
in genetics/genomics contexts, including Wikipedia/background rows. A source for
a genetics topic must carry a genetics anchor in its title or snippet before it
can enter the mini-shard; matching only generic terms such as environment,
testing, or background is insufficient.
