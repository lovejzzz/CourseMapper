# v0.15.94 Source-Finder Bycatch Cleanup Roadmap

_Written June 28, 2026. Target app version: `0.15.94`._

## Goal

Use the fresh deployed `v0.15.93` User Experience Design Studio ZIP/log audit
to remove the remaining source-finder bycatch that kept the package at `97/A`
instead of a clean `100/100`. The package had no P0/P1 findings, but six P2
citation findings remained because irrelevant source-finder candidates were
exported as `sourceReviewRows`.

Evidence folder:

`verification-output/edutool-audit-loop/20260628T084023Z-v01593-ux-fresh`

## Evidence

- Local regrade: `97/100 (A)`.
- Citation score: `82/100`.
- Texture score: `94/100`, improved from the v0.15.92 `91/100` baseline.
- Remaining findings: six citation P2s in `PACKAGE_MANIFEST.json`.
- Bad source-finder review rows included `List of Studio Ghibli works`,
  `Spiritual practice`, `Strategic planning`, `Chuck Swindoll`, and a
  pre-service-teacher peer-feedback article.
- `A/B testing` was valid UX evidence but was quarantined because the anchor
  check missed hyphenated `user-experience` text.

## Lane 1: Do Not Attach Source-Finder Bycatch

- Apply the UX relevance gate before source-finder candidates become graph
  resources.
- Do not let first-result bycatch inflate `knowledgeBackbone` open-resource
  counts.
- Prefer the first source-finder candidate that passes the course/domain gate.

## Lane 2: Do Not Export Weak Source-Finder Rows As Review Debt

- Keep false-friend source-finder candidates out of trusted `sourceLedger`
  proof.
- Also keep them out of exported `sourceReviewRows`; they are not useful
  instructor review notes when they are off-domain bycatch.
- Preserve real review debt for missing license/proof rows and for weak
  non-source-finder resources.

## Lane 3: Accept Legitimate UX Anchors

- Treat hyphenated `user-experience` evidence as a UX anchor.
- Treat concept-linked `A/B testing` and split-testing references as valid
  usability-testing evidence.
- Keep teacher-education peer-feedback papers out of trusted UX proof unless a
  future source is explicitly design-studio or UX critique focused.

## Release Boundary

This release is source retrieval/filtering truth work. It does not weaken the
grader, does not promote weak sources, and does not claim clean `100/100`
provider output. A fresh deployed `v0.15.94` ZIP/log audit is required to prove
that source-finder bycatch no longer appears as exported review rows or open
resource proof.
