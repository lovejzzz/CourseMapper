# V0.15.126 PPTX Bullet Finish Roadmap

## Goal

Prevent final exported slide decks from shipping long sentence-like body bullets
as bare clauses without terminal punctuation.

## Evidence

- App version: `0.15.125`
- Audit folder:
  `verification-output/edutool-audit-loop/20260629T110334Z-v015125-ux-fresh`
- Visible result: `Quality 99`, `Texture 95`
- Local regrade: `99/100 (A)`
- P findings: `0 P0`, `1 P1`, `0 P2`
- Export verification passed, source proof was clean, enrichment coverage was
  complete, and the package source ledger had six trusted concept-linked rows.
- The remaining scored finding was:
  - `[format] truncated slide bullet ending mid-clause without terminal punctuation`
  - file: `Slide Decks/Lesson 10 - Accessibility - Slide Decks.pptx`
  - evidence: `Accessibility is part of overall usability, not an optional add-on`

## Diagnosis

The compiler already applied a long-bullet punctuation rule on several slide
paths, but the rendered PPTX was still the final truth surface. A generated
example-slide body bullet could reach `pptxExporter` as a 60-plus-character
lowercase-ending clause and be written into slide XML without the final safety
normalization that takeaway callouts already received.

This is CourseMapper-side export hygiene. A stronger model may produce cleaner
prose more often, but final Office exports need deterministic protection against
sentence-shaped body bullets shipping as unfinished fragments.

## Lane

1. Final PPTX body-bullet safety net
   - Normalize slide bullets at `pptxExporter` render time.
   - Add terminal punctuation only for long prose bullets matching the grader's
     defect shape.
   - Preserve already punctuated bullets.

2. Shape exemptions
   - Keep short labels bare.
   - Keep relationship-arrow concept pairs such as `A ↔ B` unpunctuated.
   - Do not alter titles, slide numbers, or non-bullet metadata.

3. Regression coverage
   - Reproduce the exact v0.15.125 UX accessibility bullet.
   - Prove the rendered PPTX XML contains the terminal period.
   - Prove relationship-arrow bullet text is not over-punctuated.
