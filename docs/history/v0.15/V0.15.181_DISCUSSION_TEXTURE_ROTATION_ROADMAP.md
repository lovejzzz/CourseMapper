## Remaining Texture Loss

The local texture metric found repeated Discussion Prompt support scaffolds in all 12 discussion
documents, especially:

- `time written or spoken response options and sentence frames so students can`
- `use wait time written or spoken response options and sentence frames so`
- `written or spoken response options and sentence frames so students can cite`

The exported Office text showed this was deterministic CourseMapper compiler language, not provider
randomness:

`Equity Considerations: Use wait time, written or spoken response options, and sentence frames so
students can cite ... evidence without being rushed`.

## Goal

Move the remaining discussion support texture from one repeated compiler sentence to lesson-specific
support language before export, while keeping the same accessibility promise.

## Lane

Product/compiler texture repair. This is not a grader weakening, source-standard change, or score
inflation release.

## Release Boundary

This release may claim that:

- accessibility participation protocols vary by lesson instead of repeating one support sentence;
- discussion equity follow-up cues vary by lesson, artifact, and concept;
- the UX compiler texture regression now includes `equityConsiderations`;
- the exact v0.15.180 repeated discussion shingles are pinned by regression tests.

It may not claim a fresh deployed v0.15.181 provider score until a new ZIP/log audit proves the
deployed output.
