## Goal

Keep the clean package score honest while making the API/digest/source wording
match the exported trusted source ledger.

## Lane

Report-truth and auditability. This is not a grader weakening, source-trust
weakening, score inflation, UI redesign, or provider/model change.

## Release Boundary

This release may claim that the digest no longer renders impossible
source-backed lesson counts, and that final knowledge-backbone text separates
trusted source-ledger proof from broader graph reading resources.

It may not claim that genome coverage is solved, that every course will get a
perfect texture score, or that source-finder candidates are trusted unless they
also pass the existing real/source/on-topic/license/concept-link checks.

## V0.15.175 Scope

- Clamp source-backed judgment display counts to the course lesson denominator.
- Show trusted source-ledger rows separately from graph reading resources in
  final manifest/digest pipeline text.
- Use safer "graph reading resources" wording in generation logs before the
  source ledger is finalized.
- Add a regression for the live `19/12` digest shape.

## Why This Is CourseMapper-side

The model produced a package that passed quality and local regrade. The defect
was CourseMapper-side reporting: the readable pipeline text mixed graph-resource
candidates with trusted exported source-ledger proof and rendered an impossible
lesson coverage ratio.
