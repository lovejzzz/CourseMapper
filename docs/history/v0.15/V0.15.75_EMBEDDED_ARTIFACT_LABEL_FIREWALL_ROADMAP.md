# v0.15.75 Embedded Artifact Label Firewall Roadmap

## Evidence

A fresh deployed v0.15.74 Project Management ZIP/log audit regraded at 74/100
(C), with 1 P0, 2 P1, and 2 P2 findings. The source-ledger bridge improved on
that run: `PACKAGE_MANIFEST.json` reported 10 trusted concept-linked rows and 0
license-ambiguous rows. The highest-evidence remaining defect was prompt
artifact leakage: Course Map fields and compiled Course FAQ text treated labels
such as `course map`, `syllabus`, `lesson plans`, `slide decks`, `lesson
objectives`, and `quiz and exam bank` as Project Management concepts.

The live evidence is recorded at
`verification-output/edutool-audit-loop/20260627T-v01574-fresh/report.md`.

## Lane

Product quality and artifact-label truth. CourseMapper must deterministically
repair prompt artifact labels before export, even when a model writes them into
sentence-shaped objectives, activities, assessments, source cues, or compiled
metadata.

## Scope

- Expand the course-map readiness firewall from bare artifact labels to embedded
  labels in normal-looking lesson sentences.
- Treat CourseMapper deliverable names such as Course Map, Syllabus, Lesson
  Objectives, and Quiz & Exam Bank as unsafe student-facing concepts in normal
  disciplinary courses.
- Add compiler-side defense so stale shared lesson metadata cannot reintroduce
  these labels into Course FAQ or Study Guide output.
- Keep the grader strict by recognizing the expanded artifact-label vocabulary.

## Non-Goals

- Do not lower the P0/P1/P2 standards.
- Do not claim a clean 100/100 package before a fresh deployed ZIP/log audit.
- Do not solve partial enrichment, texture repetition, or punctuation cleanup in
  this same release.
- Do not block legitimate instructional-design courses from discussing course
  design artifacts as real content.

## Release Boundary

In scope:

- Course-map readiness repair for embedded artifact-label contamination.
- Compiler sanitization for shared Course FAQ and Study Guide grounding fields.
- Focused regression coverage based on the v0.15.74 Project Management failure.

Out of scope:

- Enrichment retry architecture.
- Broader source/research bridge work.
- Export-panel UX simplification.
- PPTX repeated phrase cleanup and double-period formatting cleanup.
