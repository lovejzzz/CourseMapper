# Trellis Roadmap v0.1.6 — Every Shelf, Every Discipline

_July 4, 2026. Input: v0.1.5's carries. The bank is deep where history
generated (cs) and family-covered where the genome documents, but
SHELF FLOORS don't exist (cs/strings starved L8 to a quiz-5), the
deepest bank has never been pointed at the discipline that needs it
most (LA's 0.43–0.48 repair plateau predates the bank entirely), and
fresh authoring at thin shelves gets no help from the 1,738 items
sitting next to it._

## 1 · Shelf floors (gap-fill round 2)

- **Finding:** family coverage ≠ depth. cs/strings had families covered
  but a shelf too thin to fill a lesson, so reviews flooded in (L8).
- **Fix:** `bankGapFill` gains floor mode: every genome kernel reaches
  ≥6 items total and ≥2 per documented family; floor items are
  application-level and family-tagged where a family is documented,
  general-application otherwise (familyKey null, still gated). Same
  gate stack, same provenance stamps. Cost-capped at $0.30.
- **Exit bar:** every kernel any fixture course maps to holds ≥6 items;
  disclosed origins in the bank header.

## 2 · The untested lever: LA on the deep bank

- **Finding:** every LA number in the ledger (repair 0.428–0.479)
  predates the bank; the math shard's families were gap-filled in
  v0.1.5 but no LA run has ever SELECTED from them.
- **Fix:** none — this is pure measurement of existing machinery.
  Same-graph replay of the frozen v012-la-verify graph with
  `--bank all`, so the delta vs its recorded 0.428 is attributable.
- **Exit bar:** LA same-graph repair ≥0.55 (honest intermediate from
  0.428; the 0.70 bar stays the destination); catch stays ≥60%.

## 3 · Exemplar-guided fresh authoring

- **Finding:** at thin shelves the fresh quiz author writes unaided
  while 1,738 gate-passed items sit unused as craft references.
- **Fix:** when a lesson authors fresh items, the quiz prompt includes
  ONE top-evidence banked item for the same kernel as a STYLE exemplar
  ("match its craft — concrete case, reason-bearing distractors; never
  copy its content"). Zero new calls; ~200 extra input tokens.
- **Exit bar:** L8 and L13 quiz panels ≥6.5 on the bench11 replay
  (L8 was 5, L13 was 6).

## 4 · Blend acceptance re-measure

- **Finding:** 88% measured before the 80-word/ds tail landed; never
  re-measured cleanly.
- **Fix:** none — read the blend ratios from this release's two
  validation replays.
- **Exit bar:** ≥90% combined on both, or the honest number with the
  residual class named.

## Validation design

Two frozen rulers, one fresh probe: bench11 cs replay (floors +
exemplars; panel for L8/L13 bars) and v012-la-verify LA replay (the
lever measurement). Estimated spend ≤$0.75 (floors ~$0.30, two replays
~$0.35, panel ~$0.02). Standing discipline: suite green per commit,
§20 per milestone, overruns disclosed.
