# v0.15.32 Slide Note Echo Guard Roadmap

## Evidence

Fresh deployed EduTool.dev audit:

- run folder: `verification-output/edutool-audit-loop/20260620T120411Z`
- app version: `0.15.31`
- prompt: four-week undergraduate Environmental Justice and Climate Policy package
- visible badge: `Quality 99 · Texture 94`
- local regrade: `99/100 (A)`
- digest: `run-1781957225253`, 6 provider calls, `$0.08`

The audit showed that v0.15.31 shipped the source-review truth fix: `SL1`
moved to `sourceReviewRows` and `SOURCE_REPORT.md` review notes instead of a
trusted bibliography row. The remaining new product defect was a slide-deck
format P2:

`Slide Decks/Lesson 04 - Policy memo methods - Slide Decks.pptx` rendered an
`X: X` chain around `Environmental Justice and Climate Policy: Policy memo
methods...` in title-slide text/speaker notes. The same deck also inherited a
numbered assessment echo from the source assessment label.

## Goal

Keep slide decks lesson-specific without course-title echo chains. Course names
belong in package/deck metadata and high-level context, not repeated inside
title-slide anchors where they form `Course: Topic` patterns.

## Lane

Narrow compiler/export-text cleanup. This release does not claim 100/100 and
does not address the remaining trusted-source-ledger debt; that requires the
ResearchSession/CourseIR source bridge.

## Plan

1. Build title-slide context from the lesson concept phrase, not
   `courseName: phrase`.
2. De-duplicate `Assessment title: 1. Assessment title` before using the
   assessment label as a slide artifact.
3. Sanitize title-slide note anchors so long course-name prefixes are stripped
   before they enter quoted `Use "..."` note text.
4. Add an Environmental Justice / Policy memo compiler regression over the
   slide surface that is actually exported to PPTX: titles, bullets, visual
   descriptors, visual plans, and speaker notes.

## Non-Goals

- No grader weakening or score inflation.
- No new live provider call after the fix until v0.15.32 deploys; this release
  is based on the v0.15.31 ZIP/log evidence.
- No claim that the source-review row is solved as trusted bibliography.

## Release Boundary

Ship only if the focused compiler regression, format, lint, full test suite,
build, bundle check, release-history audit, gold smoke, and browser
verification pass locally. After push, Fast verification and Pages deploy must
also complete before the release is claimed.
