# v0.15.89 Source Proof Hardening Roadmap

_Written June 28, 2026. Target app version: `0.15.89`._

## Goal

Move CourseMapper closer to clean `100/100` exports by making source proof stricter and more honest. A row in `sourceLedger` should mean trusted bibliography proof: accessible, license-safe, trust-eligible, and linked to course concepts. Anything weaker should remain visible as instructor review evidence without inflating citation quality.

## Lane 1: Trusted Source Ledger Contract

- Require trusted ledger rows to satisfy the same proof shape the deep grader expects: URL or DOI, non-ambiguous license, trust-eligible provider, and concept links.
- Quarantine CourseIR, instructor, metadata-only, missing-license, or unlinked rows as `sourceReviewRows`.
- Preserve review visibility in `SOURCE_REPORT.md` instead of hiding weak rows.

## Lane 2: Concept-Link Recovery

- When a resource is attached to a lesson section but the graph has no explicit concept ids, use the section topic as a concept label.
- Keep explicit concept ids preferred whenever they exist.
- Prevent sourceRef coverage from being bridged through trusted-but-unlinked rows.

## Lane 3: Duplicate Source Notes

- Merge trusted source rows across the full package before adding review rows.
- Suppress weaker review duplicates when a stronger trusted row with the same DOI or URL already exists.
- Keep distinct review-only candidates visible when they do not duplicate trusted proof.

## Release Boundary

This release does not claim clean package quality. It hardens the source proof contract and should be followed by a fresh deployed `v0.15.89` ZIP/log audit to measure whether citation score improves in real Project Management and UX-style provider output.
