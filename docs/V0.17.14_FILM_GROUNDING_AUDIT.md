# EDUTOOL v0.17.14 — Film Grounding and Score-Honesty Audit

## Goal

Prevent a wrong-discipline source from teaching through an otherwise coherent package, and make the quality score expose the exact recurrence if it ever returns.

## Lane

Source admission, graph-to-compiler evidence safety, and deterministic score honesty. This lane does not change model weights or claim broad pedagogical improvement.

## Decision

Ship **v0.17.14 — Discipline Before Keywords** as a narrow learner-safety and score-honesty release.

## Release Boundary

The release is earned when the audited film/music-metre failure is rejected before compilation, its mutation produces a discipline P0, the unchanged production ZIP regrades below B, and the full local release gates pass. Deployment and remote exact-commit checks remain separate post-push evidence.

The production v0.17.13 package is not a quality checkpoint. It is valuable negative evidence: an ostensibly aligned film course taught music metre and poetry prosody in its film-editing lesson while the package grader awarded 89/B and discipline 100. V0.17.14 prevents that exact source class from reaching compilation and makes the unchanged bad ZIP score 74/C with a discipline P0.

## Real course audited

- Course: **Film Form and Cultural Analysis**
- Source: deployed `https://edutool.dev/`, app v0.17.13
- Scope: 10 undergraduate humanities lessons, screenings, shot analysis, response papers, and a final scene analysis
- ZIP SHA-256: `664ac7a233ff1b14b935870559f81d22b3ea556169a271c90fdb8a2b690db9c1`
- ZIP bytes: `1,901,812`
- Package files: 98 entries, 83 learner/instructor content files

The package’s deterministic evidence report earned **51/100**, while package conformance reported **89/B**. Only **1 of 10** lessons had byte-bound exact source claims.

## What failed

Lesson 3, “Editing rhythms,” retrieved and retained:

- `Metre (music)`
- `Rhythm`
- `Metre (poetry)` as a review-only overlay

That evidence propagated Indian tala, strong/weak beat meter, hypermetre, additive rhythm, divisive rhythm, and poetry prosody across 8–9 artifacts. The lesson’s objectives, activities, assessment, rubric, handout, lesson plan, and slides then aligned coherently around the wrong domain.

This was not only provider noise. The product made three deterministic mistakes:

1. Retrieval treated the overloaded lesson words “rhythm” and “meter” as stronger identity than the film course.
2. The knowledge backbone attached research citations before applying the shared course-aware lesson gate.
3. The compiler constructed its enrichment from the original graph after creating a safer resource view, restoring evidence that the safety pass had already rejected.

The scorer made a fourth mistake: music contamination vocabulary covered counterpoint, fugue, and harmonic intervals, but not metre, tala, or prosody. Its default-100-minus-known-findings design therefore reported discipline 100.

## Roundtable consultation

Roundtable session `0adbd954-73cd-40b0-9677-ebb9a6958760` audited repository code and the fresh production evidence. The room completed seven turns including steering. Claude, Antigravity, and Fable became unavailable during the run, so Roundtable continued in resilient mode with two independent Codex audits and one completion brief; this limitation is part of the evidence, not hidden.

The final decision was to treat v0.17.13 as failing semantic grounding and score honesty, reject bad evidence before compilation, and convert the exact recurrence into a score-bearing P0. The room also identified visual-usability scoring as unfinished: dense DOCX tables, a sparse fifth page, and sterile text-heavy slides were not reflected in the format score.

## Implemented

### Admission

- Source Finder cache/version advances to v12.
- Film course identity disables the generic music-rhythm shortcut.
- Film candidates require a moving-image anchor such as film, cinema, shot, editing, montage, cinematography, camera, or soundtrack.
- The shared source ledger identifies film-editing metre false friends while allowing genuinely film-contextual interdisciplinary sources.

### Propagation

- Algi/genome citations pass the shared course-aware source gate before becoming graph resources.
- A failed citation records a deterministic rejection decision.
- The compiler safe graph removes course-aware weak resources.
- If one citation makes a lesson evidence set non-separable, the whole lesson enrichment payload is quarantined.
- Blueprint enrichment is rebuilt from the safe graph, not the unsafe original.

### Scoring

- Deep grader 1.16.0 recognizes the audited music/poetry-metre contamination in a film-editing lesson as P0.
- Regrading the unchanged production artifact changes overall conformance **89/B → 74/C** and discipline **100 → 75**.
- This is an honesty improvement, not a claim that the old package became better.

## Proof

The focused suite covers four boundaries:

1. retrieval rejects `Metre (music)` and `Metre (poetry)` while retaining `Film editing`;
2. resource attachment rejects a researched music-metre citation for a film-editing lesson;
3. compiler output contains none of the rejected metre/tala facts;
4. the rendered-package grader detects the exact contamination and prevents an A.

The production artifact is independently regraded through the Office-byte reader, not a compiler-only object fixture.

## Honest limits and next work

V0.17.14 does not certify factual accuracy, pedagogy, accessibility, instructor approval, or classroom outcomes. It does not make the v0.17.13 film package teachable; it prevents the diagnosed source path in future builds and scores the retained failure honestly.

The next quality work should address:

- rendered DOCX page-density and trailing-page measurements;
- semantic visual requirements for disciplines such as film, where circles and text boxes are not substitutes for shot evidence, timelines, or frame analysis;
- source-to-claim entailment beyond exact copied spans;
- blinded instructor review across several unfamiliar disciplines and providers.
