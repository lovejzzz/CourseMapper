# Composer Roadmap v0.2.5 — Echo & Fresh Ground

_July 5, 2026. Input: E7d's residual (J7 echo ×2, and $0.119 of repair
grinding on it) and the standing self-reuse validity caveat._

## 1 · Course-level asset dedup (echo dies at selection)

- **Finding:** J7 echo in composed courses is the same asset (or a
  > 0.8 sibling) selected into multiple lessons — per-lesson exclusion
  > plus exposure counters reduce but don't prevent recurrence on thin
  > shelves.
- **Fix:** a course-level used-set threaded through composition; an
  asset never appears twice in one course. Thin-shelf fallback permits
  reuse and COUNTS it (`dupReuses`, disclosed in the digest).
- **Exit bar:** J7 ≤1 per composed course without repair's help.

## 2 · Composed repair skips echo

- **Finding:** the repair loop spent $0.119 rewriting echo it cannot
  fix (cross-lesson phrasing identity is a composition property).
- **Fix:** composed runs exclude J7_ECHO from repair-triggering;
  echo findings remain in the honest residual and in J-counts.
- **Exit bar:** E7e (LA re-measure) total ≤$0.12, repair ≤$0.05,
  battery within the v1.2 band (≥0.50 after the 0.553 result).

## 3 · E8 — fresh ground (the self-reuse caveat test)

- **Finding:** every Composer panel so far reused assets harvested
  partly from the very graphs being replayed — the honest best case.
- **Method:** FRESH cs intake (new graph the library has never seen),
  full composition, battery + 3-seat panel.
- **Exit bars:** grade ≥97 · panel ≥7.5 · cost ≤$0.10 · J7 ≤2. Misses
  reported as the true generalization number.

## Budget

≤$0.45 (E7e ~$0.13, E8 ~$0.10, one panel ~$0.025).
