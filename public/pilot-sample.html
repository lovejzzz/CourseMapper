Input

Course brief

Practical AI Literacy for Educators — a four-week professional-learning course for university instructors. One 90-minute session per week. Participants should learn to evaluate AI outputs, design responsible classroom activities, and document their use of AI.

Deliverable 1

Course map

Swipe horizontally to view every column.

Week Topic Learning objective In-session activity Evidence of learning
1 How generative AI works Explain how a generative model produces an answer and identify two limitations. Annotate a sample response for pattern completion, uncertainty, and missing context. A 150-word explanation plus two correctly identified limitations.
2 Evaluating AI output Apply a repeatable evaluation checklist to an AI-generated teaching artifact. Audit a sample lesson plan for accuracy, bias, privacy, and pedagogical fit. An evidence-backed audit with pass-or-revise decisions.
3 Designing responsible activities Design an AI-supported activity that preserves meaningful student judgment. Redesign a weak “ask AI and submit” task using staged decisions and process evidence. An activity brief with a goal, learner actions, safeguards, and assessment criteria.
4 Classroom policy and disclosure Create a concise course policy defining permitted use and disclosure expectations. Compare three policy scenarios, then draft and peer-review a local policy. A one-page policy and completed peer-review checklist.

Deliverable 2

Lesson plan — Week 2: Evaluating AI output

Duration
90 minutes
Objective
Apply a four-part checklist and justify each decision with observable evidence.

Swipe horizontally to view every column.

Time Activity Instructor action Participant action Check for understanding
0–10 Retrieval warm-up Display two short AI claims, one supportable and one uncertain. Mark what they would verify first and explain why. Separate fluent wording from evidence.
10–25 Checklist model Think aloud while auditing one paragraph. Record the evidence used for each checklist dimension. Name evidence, not just a rating.
25–50 Group audit Distribute a synthetic AI-generated lesson plan. Audit accuracy, bias, privacy, and pedagogical fit. Record one pass and one revise decision with justification.
50–68 Cross-group challenge Pair groups and assign a skeptical reviewer role. Challenge one unsupported decision and revise the audit. The revised decision cites a concrete feature.
68–82 Individual transfer Provide a second, shorter artifact. Complete an individual audit. Address all four dimensions and distinguish known from uncertain.
82–90 Exit ticket Ask what could make a factually correct AI output unsuitable. Submit a two-sentence answer. Identify a pedagogical, privacy, or fairness concern.

Materials
